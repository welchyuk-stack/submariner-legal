Common questions

Why can't my diver jump?
He's in a heavy brass suit. Hold Float to pump air in and rise, and let go to sink. Getting up there is the puzzle.

I ran out of air.
Each level has one vent that refills your tank. If you're stranded, tap Retry and you'll carry on from your last refill with every gem you've found.

I can't find the last gem or pearl.
Turn the diorama. Some routes and pearls are only visible from certain angles.

My progress isn't on my other device.
Progress syncs through iCloud. Check you're signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on for both devices, then open the game on each.

Where does my data go?
Nowhere the developer can see. See the privacy policy.
